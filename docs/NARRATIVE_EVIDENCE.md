# Narrative evidence policy

Accepted campaign order: Nemesis -> Nemesis 2 -> Salamander -> Nemesis 3.
The final chapter's travel into the past stays inside the final chapter; sorting
every scene by its calendar date would reveal and disrupt that story structure.

The earlier conversational synopsis is **not** a primary source. Dates, ages,
character relationships, exact translations, mission ordering and ending
conditions from that synopsis must not become final cutscene text without
checking manuals, original in-game text and the game's event conditions.

Record each scene with: source game/revision; original text and language;
ROM bank/address or manual page; triggering condition; translation; adaptation
notes; assets/music; and persistent flags. New connective scenes must be marked
as editorial adaptation rather than attributed to Konami.

Preserve alternate endings and optional discoveries. Do not silently force the
good route by setting a cheat flag. Integrate Salamander's cross-cartridge
content into the single campaign while retaining its gameplay discovery logic.

No complete narrative script or verified calendar timeline has been extracted
in CP0001. The campaign contract is an implementation requirement, not proof
that all cutscenes already exist.
